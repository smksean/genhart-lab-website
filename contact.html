FAQ

Do I need to be advanced before joining?

No. You need some exposure to Python, data analysis, or machine learning, plus willingness to build consistently.

Is this a beginner course?

Not exactly. It is for learners ready to build applied projects, portfolio proof, and interview confidence.

Will I get a certificate?

The main value is project evidence: GitHub repositories, reports, demos, case studies, and clearer explanations.

What kind of projects will I build?

Projects may come from engineering, healthcare, finance, e-commerce, marketing, or general applied ML.

Will this guarantee me a job?

No honest programme can guarantee that. The goal is stronger proof, better skills, and clearer presentation.

How much time do I need weekly?

Plan for 6 to 10 hours per week, including sessions, project work, and portfolio development.
